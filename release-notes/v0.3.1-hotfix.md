Phase 3.1: a hotfix for what testing of 0.3.0 turned up, plus the requested road, sign, light and water features. Linux AppImage only.

**Loading**

- Road data comes in smaller pieces (about 1 km instead of 2 km), nearest first, so the streets around you appear sooner
- The public Overpass servers are raced: if the first hasn't answered in 8 seconds the next one starts, and the first good answer wins. If every Overpass server fails, the main OpenStreetMap API fills in
- Road data is cached in a new format (it now includes water, freeway exits, mile markers, level crossings and power lines), so areas you visited in 0.3.0 download once more

**Roads**

- The terrain no longer pokes up through roads and sidewalks: roads are built from denser rows that follow the ground closely, and the levelled strip under them now includes the sidewalks
- Curbs on city streets wherever there are sidewalks, including where the sidewalks are mapped separately
- Roads that gain or lose a lane widen or narrow smoothly instead of stepping
- Big intersections of divided roads are one intersection: one paved surface, one set of signals in step with each other
- Sidewalks sit level with the road edge on hills, and are only assumed on town streets (not on fast, rural or unpaved roads)
- Raised freeways through cities stand on retaining walls instead of raising the ground around them; underpasses still dip down as before
- Bridge parapets open where on-ramps merge, and guardrails stop short of side roads and ramps, so ramps no longer run into a wall
- Construction-zone roads look like the road they belong to; cones and barrels stand on the road surface and follow its curves

**Markings and lights**

- Turn arrows at their real size and shape
- Traffic signals show red, amber and green (they were glowing white)
- Pedestrian signals show the raised hand and the walking person
- Streetlights light the street below them as pools of light that you can see from far away, and more of the nearby lamps cast real light
- Nights are much darker, so streetlights and signals carry the scene, with a soft glow (bloom) around bright lights

**Bridges, signs and objects**

- Bridges follow their type in the map: girder bridges on concrete pier bents, steel truss bridges, arch bridges, suspension bridges with towers and cables, and cable-stayed bridges, painted in their mapped colour (the Golden Gate is orange)
- Overhead guide signs before freeway exits, on gantries over the road or on the side of an overpass
- Mile markers along freeways, from the map or worked out from the exit numbers
- Street name signs: the pole no longer pokes through the blades
- Licence-plate cameras look like the real Flock units: slim pole, solar panel, camera on an arm
- Utility poles with wires along streets, and fire hydrants at the curb, where the map doesn't have them
- Streetlights, bus stop signs, pedestrian signals and other objects face the street the right way
- Railroad crossings: the rails sit flush in concrete crossing panels, with crossbuck signs (with lights where the crossing has them)

**Water**

- Lakes, reservoirs and rivers show as water with moving ripples instead of flat imagery, and you can swim in them

**Known limits**

- Bridge types come from the map's `bridge:structure` tag; untagged bridges are drawn as girder bridges
- Mile markers are only worked out where exit numbers follow mileposts
- Lakes are as deep as a swimming pool for now
