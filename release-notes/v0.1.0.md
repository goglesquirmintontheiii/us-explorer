Phase 1: terrain, imagery and streaming.

**New**

- Ground detail when you're close: procedural grass, asphalt, dirt and rock blended under the imagery, with small bumps that catch the light
- Rock on steep slopes, so canyon walls and cliffs no longer show stretched imagery
- Sharper ground on the High preset (imagery up to zoom 19, about 0.25 m per pixel)
- Imagery textures are BC1-compressed on GPUs that support it, using about 8x less video memory
- Teleporting holds you 350 m above the ground until detailed terrain arrives, instead of dropping you at ground level
- Settings, Credits and Offline download windows open in the middle of the screen
- The overlay shows the zoom the imagery under you really came from

**Fixed**

- Downloads through GitHub's redirects (updates) could fail on some networks
- The game now trusts your system's certificate store, so it works behind proxies and firewalls with their own certificates

**Known limits**

- Lakes and coasts are flat at sea level until water arrives in Phase 5
- Snow on peaks follows the weather, which arrives in Phase 7

If updating from v0.0.0 fails, download this AppImage from the release page; later updates use the fixed downloader.
