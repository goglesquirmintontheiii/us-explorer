Phase 5: nature, water and the sky. Also the fixes and requests from testing 0.4.0. Linux AppImage and Windows .exe.

**Trees, grass and ground cover**

- Trees match where you are: 51 species in regional mixes (palms in South Florida and on warm beaches, live oaks and cedar in the Texas Hill Country, pines and firs in the West and North, maples and oaks in the East, saguaros and Joshua trees in the deserts), chosen by setting too: street trees in town, woods, riverside and coast
- Every tree is built procedurally at one of four growth stages, with real branching, bark and 3D leaves; far away they turn into flat cards. Below the default graphics preset (Low and Medium) trees and grass are 2D
- Ground cover is read from the aerial imagery and the map: woods, lawns, dry grass, shrubs, crops in rows, wetlands, sand, dunes and rock. Grass is 3D blades at High and Ultra, coloured from the imagery
- Seasons come from NASA satellite greenness: trees leaf out, turn colour and stand bare when the real ones do, and dry summers brown the grass

**Water**

- The ocean has real waves: swell and wind waves from the Open-Meteo marine forecast (checked against the nearest NOAA buoy), growing steeper as they reach shallow water, with whitecaps in strong wind and surf breaking on the beach
- Beaches have wet and dry sand, foam, dune grass behind them, piers, and lifeguard towers
- Buoys and beacons where they really are (OpenStreetMap sea marks), in the right colours and shapes, bobbing on the waves, their lights flashing in their charted rhythm
- Lakes ripple with the real wind; rivers flow downstream; streams and small rivers are drawn too

**Details**

- Fences, walls, hedges, power lines on lattice, steel and wooden towers, park benches and tables
- Wind turbines turn into the real wind, faster when it blows harder
- Parked cars in parking lots and along residential streets: sedans, SUVs, pickups, hatchbacks and minivans in the colours American cars come in

**The sky**

- The real moon, in its real phase, lighting the night; 7,000 real stars and the planets where they are tonight; the Milky Way
- Light pollution from NASA's night-lights map: in a city you see a few bright stars under an orange glow, in the countryside the Milky Way; cloud cover hides them
- The northern lights, from NOAA's aurora forecast, when they're out where you are
- Nights are much darker
- The overlay shows your local time, with time zones and daylight saving
- Ambient sound: the city's hum, highway traffic, wind in the trees, birds by day, crickets on warm nights, surf, and wind up high. Turn it off or set the volume in Settings

**Fixes and requests from 0.4.0**

- Fixed the crash after a few minutes inside a house (the bathroom mirror and interior shadows were overloading the GPU). If the game ever crashes, a crash log is saved and the title screen tells you where it is
- Fixed the freeze when a large building's interior loads
- Stores now differ: 19 kinds of store with their own fixtures, goods, floors and colours
- The title screen always opens and is very light (it no longer draws the world behind it, and runs at 30 fps)
- Parking lots lie flat on the ground with no raised corners, their stripes no longer sink into the surface, and wheel stops and accessible-parking signs show up
- Streets in downtown San Antonio no longer rise several metres above the River Walk, and rivers are no longer drawn at street level
- Divided roads no longer get sidewalks and curbs in the median overlapping each other
- Pedestrian signals face the people waiting to cross, and bus stop signs face approaching traffic

**Settings and credits**

- New settings: nature, details (fences, power lines, turbines, buoys, piers), ambient sound and volume
- Offline downloads include the nature layer
- Credits for Open-Meteo, NOAA (buoys, space weather), NASA GIBS (greenness, night lights), the Yale Bright Star Catalogue and JPL planetary positions
