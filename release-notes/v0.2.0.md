Phase 2: player, cameras and maps.

**New**

- You have a body now: walk, run, jump, fall, hover, fly and swim animations, and four outfits (Settings → Outfit: casual, rain jacket, winter, hiker)
- Three camera views, cycled with V (or F5): first person with your body visible, third person over the shoulder, and a free orbit camera (the mouse orbits, the wheel zooms). The third-person and orbit cameras pull in instead of going through cliffs and slopes
- Walking with gravity: jump with Space, step up curbs, stick to the ground going downhill
- Double-tap Space (within 0.3 s) to switch between walking and flying. Flying: Space rises, Shift descends, and you stop as soon as you let go
- Sprint with Ctrl in both modes; the mouse wheel sets fly speed from walking pace up to 1,000 km/h
- Flying down onto the ground drops you back into walking (Settings → Land on touch)
- Swimming in the sea: wade in the shallows, float at the surface in deeper water, Space swims up and Shift dives
- No-clip with N, for getting unstuck
- Corner minimap that turns with you; click it or press M for the full map
- Full map: drag and zoom out to the whole country, search for any place or address, double-click (or right-click → Teleport here) to go there
- Bookmarks (B, or the map): saved with a name looked up from the location and the way you were facing; recent places are listed too
- Gamepad support: left stick moves, right stick looks, A jumps (double-tap to fly), B goes down, Y changes camera, X bookmarks, Select opens the map
- Every key can be rebound (Settings → Controls)

**Fixed**

- BC1 texture compression really is on now. v0.1.0 listed it, but the check that turns it on never ran, so imagery used about 8x more video memory than it needed
- Death Valley, the Salton Sea basin and New Orleans were drawn at sea level; they're below it now

**Changed**

- The weather service is built into the game, so there's one file to download
- Windows: one-click updates replace the .exe in place, and there's no console window

**Known limits**

- Buildings arrive in Phase 4, and with them collision against walls (no-clip is there for now)
- Map layers for radar, clouds, warnings, hurricanes, fires, smoke and aircraft are shown but switched off until their phases
- Inland lakes and rivers aren't water yet (Phase 5), so you can only swim in the sea
