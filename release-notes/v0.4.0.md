Phase 4: buildings, with interiors you can walk into. Also the fixes and requests from testing 0.3.1. Linux AppImage and Windows .exe.

**Buildings**

- Every building in OpenStreetMap is drawn in 3D: heights from the map (or sensible defaults by type and size), multi-part buildings with setbacks and towers, and roofs in their mapped shape — gabled, hipped, gambrel, mansard, pyramidal, skillion, round, domes — with eaves, chimneys, parapets and rooftop equipment
- Roof colours come from the map, or are measured from the aerial imagery over each roof
- Walls are textured by building type and region: brick with mortar joints, siding, stucco, stone, concrete, glass curtain walls with inset panes, 3D shingles. Windows have frames and a room behind the glass, shops have storefronts, and windows light up at night
- Houses get porches, chimneys, garages and carports, and doors in many styles and colours that suit the house
- Buildings sit on the ground properly: foundations reach down to the terrain on slopes, and the terrain never shows inside a building or its basement
- Landmark models: put a glTF file named after a building's OSM id (for example `way34633854.glb` for the Empire State Building) in the `landmarks` folder in the game's data folder, and it replaces that building, placed and scaled automatically

**Doors and interiors**

- Real doors at the mapped entrances (or on the street side). Right-click a door to open or close it; double doors open together
- Buildings close to you that you can see into get a full interior, furnished for what the building is: houses (living rooms, kitchens with tile or wood floors, bedrooms, bathrooms, laundry, garages with boxes, attics with insulation, ducts and a water heater, basements finished or bare with exposed pipes and wiring), offices (lobbies, stairs and elevators, open-plan cubicles, offices, conference rooms, break rooms with kitchens, printers, scanners, phones and computers), shops and supermarkets (checkouts, shelving, fridge cases, produce, no brand names), restaurants by kind (fast food, cafes, bars, sit-down and fine dining), schools, hospitals, hotels, churches, factories (machines, conveyor belts, lockers) and warehouses (pallet racks)
- Where the map has indoor rooms mapped (OSM Simple Indoor Tagging, as in some famous buildings), those are used
- Stairs between floors, and elevators that work: call buttons on each floor, floor buttons in the car
- Right-click a chair, sofa or toilet to sit (move to get up), a bed to lie down. Turn on taps, showers and baths and watch them run and fill; flush toilets; open fridges, freezers, ovens, microwaves, dishwashers and washing machines; switch screens on
- Bathroom mirrors show a real reflection, you included
- Rooms have their own paint colours and floors, ceiling lights that light them, outlets, light switches and Ethernet ports
- Indoors your eyes adjust to the room light

**Parking lots**

- Parking lots are paved, with rows of striped stalls laid along the mapped aisles, accessible stalls with hatched access aisles and signs nearest the buildings, planted islands and medians, wheel stops and light poles

**Fixes from 0.3.1 testing**

- The terrain no longer pokes through roads, and roads passing under other roads load properly instead of collapsing to the lowest one
- The Golden Gate is drawn as one bridge (not two overlapping)
- Signs and other objects no longer stand in the middle of roads
- Green signal lights are green, pedestrian signal symbols are readable in daylight, and far lights no longer flicker
- More realistic licence-plate cameras
- You can no longer swim through the bottom of lakes, and water colour follows the region and kind of water (blue, green, brownish)
- Far water no longer looks like white noise
- The ground no longer turns red at high altitude
- The mouse cursor is freed when the map is open, and map tiles no longer stay empty until you zoom
- A title screen: Play (choose where to start), Resume (where you left off), Customize (edit your character) and Settings
- GPU usage in the F3 overlay
- Flight speed goes up to Mach 10

**Known limits**

- Interiors are generated from the building's shape and type; only buildings with mapped indoor rooms follow their real layout
- Tall buildings load six floors at a time around the floor you're on
- Landmark models have no way in (they block like walls)
- Computers turn on but don't run anything yet (that comes in a later phase)
